Privacy Policy

Last updated September 18, 2026

Job Claim Check is operated by Lwin Solutions Pro LLC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect when you use this site, and — because it's the reason this service exists — exactly who we share it with.

Job Claim Check is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice. We connect people with independent attorneys who pay to advertise with us.

What we collect

  • What you tell us: your name, phone number, email, state, and your answers about what happened at work.
  • Your consent record: the consent language you agreed to, when you agreed, and the technical details of that moment (including your IP address and the page you were on), captured by our compliance provider so there is a verifiable record of your permission.
  • Technical data: browser type, device, and how you reached this page (for example, which ad), collected through cookies and similar technologies including advertising pixels.

Who we share it with

If your situation fits, we send your information to one independent law firm that handles that kind of matter in your state, so they can contact you. That firm pays us for the introduction. We send each person's information to one firm only — we do not sell or share it with multiple firms.

We also use service providers who help us run the site (hosting, our customer database, and consent-recording services), only as needed to operate. We do not sell your information to data brokers or unrelated marketers.

We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect our rights or the safety of others.

Calls and text messages

If you agree on our form, we and the participating law firm may contact you by phone, email, and text message about your inquiry. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any text to opt out, or HELP for help. Agreeing is not a condition of any purchase or service.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text-messaging opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third party, other than the one participating law firm that contacts you about your inquiry.

Advertising and cookies

We use advertising technologies, including the Meta Pixel, to measure whether our ads work. We do not send your quiz answers to advertising platforms. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

How long we keep it, and your choices

We keep information as long as needed for the purposes above and to maintain consent records required by law. You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your information, or to stop contacting you, at any time using the contact details below.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards, including encrypted connections and access-controlled systems. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Children

This site is for adults 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.
